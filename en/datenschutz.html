Privacy Policy

We are delighted that you are interested in our website. Protecting your privacy is very important to us. Below we inform you in detail about how we handle your personal data.

1. Controller

The controller responsible for data collection and processing on this website is:

Kufsteiner Denkmal-Fassaden- und Gebäudereinigung GmbH
Fransa De Perea 18, 37000, Kruševac
E-mail: info@kufsteiner-reinigung.at
Phone: +43 123 456 789

If you have any questions regarding data protection, you can contact us at any time.

2. Collection and processing of data

Every time you access our website, information of a general nature is automatically collected. This information (server log files) includes, for example, the browser type, the operating system used, the domain name of your internet service provider, the date and time of access and the page requested. This data is technically necessary in order to correctly deliver the content of our website and to ensure stability and security. These data are not evaluated for personal purposes.

In addition, we collect personal data only if you provide it voluntarily via our contact form or by e-mail (e.g. name, e-mail address, telephone number). These data are used exclusively to process your request or to fulfil contractual obligations.

The legal basis for processing is Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR (performance of a contract) and Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR (legitimate interests, e.g. the security of the website).

3. Cookies

Our website uses cookies to improve user-friendliness and to provide certain functions (e.g. the booking form). Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device. You can disable the use of cookies in your browser settings. Please note that this may limit the functionality of the website.

We use the following types of cookies:

  • Necessary cookies: These cookies are required for the operation of the website (e.g. storing your language settings). They are processed on the basis of Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR.
  • Analytics cookies: We may use tools such as Google Analytics to collect usage data (e.g. page views). Wherever possible, this data is anonymised. You can object to this processing by disabling cookies or activating your browser's tracking protection.

Further information can be found in our cookie banner and in the settings of your browser.

4. Contact form

If you send us enquiries via the contact form or by e-mail, the information you provide in the enquiry form (e.g. name, e-mail address, telephone number, date, requested service) including the contact details you provide there will be stored by us for the purpose of processing the enquiry and in case of follow-up questions. We do not pass on these data without your consent.

The data will be stored for the duration of the processing of your enquiry and then deleted in accordance with statutory retention periods (e.g. 7 years for tax purposes) or until you withdraw your consent, provided there is no further obligation to retain the data.

5. Data sharing

We only share your personal data with third parties if this is required by law (e.g. to authorities) or if you have explicitly consented to it. Data processing in countries outside the EU (third countries) does not take place unless this is expressly stated separately (e.g. when using international service providers).

6. Security measures

We implement technical and organisational measures to protect your data against unauthorised access, loss or misuse. Our website is protected by SSL/TLS encryption to ensure the confidentiality of your data during transmission.

7. Your rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of access to the data stored about you, its origin and recipients and the purpose of storage.
  • Right to rectification of incorrect data or completion of incomplete data.
  • Right to erasure of your data, provided that there are no legal retention obligations (e.g. tax law).
  • Right to restriction of processing under certain conditions.
  • Right to data portability (in a structured, commonly used and machine-readable format).
  • Right to object to the processing of your data.
  • Right to lodge a complaint with the Austrian Data Protection Authority (www.dsb.gv.at).

To exercise your rights, please contact us by e-mail at info@kufsteiner-reinigung.at or by post at the address given above.

8. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We reserve the right to amend this Privacy Policy from time to time so that it always complies with current legal requirements or to reflect changes to our services in the Privacy Policy (e.g. the introduction of new services). The current version is always available on this page.
